DETECTGPT

Detecting and Interpreting Text Generated by Large Language Models

Large language models can generate text that closely resembles human writing, making reliable detection difficult. This project investigates different approaches to detecting machine-generated text, while also considering how detection can work across languages and how the evidence behind a prediction can be made visible.

Project Overview

DetectGPT brings together three individual honours investigations into machine-generated-text detection. Rather than forcing the three studies into a single benchmark, the website presents each contribution on its own research page, with its own aims, methods, results and supporting material.
